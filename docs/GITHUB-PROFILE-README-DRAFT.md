# Zulqarnain Yusuf Nadabo

**Institutional Architect & Digital Systems Strategist**

I work at the intersection of institutional design, digital systems and evidence-led development.

My work focuses on translating organisational and community problems into structures, workflows and digital products that can be examined, improved and used.

## Areas of work
- **Institutional architecture:** governance requirements, operating models and organisational systems.
- **Digital systems:** product framing, workflow design and implementation coordination.
- **Civic intelligence:** community records, public evidence, source provenance and accountability.
- **Spatial intelligence:** geospatial evidence integrity and evaluation.

## Working principles
- Separate reports, observations, evidence and verified outcomes.
- Preserve source provenance and disclose limitations.
- Keep human review in consequential publication workflows.
- Build technology around a defined institutional need.
